The verdict總結論

The wiki is in better shape than expected. On Results and Engineering the live pages are a superset of the local drafts — they carry extra timepoints, p-values, reconciled contradictions and caveats the drafts never had. On Plant, every agar set, all six hydroponic runs and all three soil runs are published with their figures. Measurement transferred nearly completely. Wiki 的狀況比預期好。 Results 和 Engineering 兩頁其實是本地草稿的超集——線上多了額外的時間點、p 值、把草稿裡自相矛盾的數字並列處理，以及草稿沒有的但書。Plant 頁把每一組 agar、六次水耕、三次土耕全部連圖發表了。Measurement 幾乎完整轉移。

What is genuinely absent is narrower than feared, but one loss is serious: an entire chapter of original assay-chemistry work was deleted on 21 October and survives only as a dangling citation. That chapter, not any plant result, is the strongest unpublished material the team has. 真正缺的比擔心的少，但有一項損失很嚴重：10 月 21 日整章原創的分析化學工作被刪除，線上只剩一條孤立的參考文獻。那一章，而不是任何植物結果，才是這個隊伍最強的未發表素材。

Five things worth the video值得放進影片的五件事

Ranked by what they add to the judged story, after checking each claim against the live page text.依照對評審故事的貢獻排序；每一項都已逐條回查線上頁面文字確認。

1

ACC is itself chromogenic — the Berthelot chapterACC 本身就會顯色——被刪掉的 Berthelot 一章

Measurement · deleted 21 Oct, commit 9f0c7bc5 · cut on purposemoderate evidence

Standard curves run with and without 2 mM ACC: the intercept rises 0.068 → 0.418. That 0.350 corresponds to 669 µM of apparent NH₄⁺, i.e. ACC reads as roughly 30% as chromogenic as ammonium, and the slope is suppressed 9.4%. The consequence is blunt: a plain NH₄Cl calibration underestimates ACC-deaminase product by about 34%. 在有無 2 mM ACC 兩種條件下做標準曲線：截距從 0.068 升到 0.418。這 0.350 相當於 669 µM 的表觀 NH₄⁺，也就是說 ACC 的顯色能力大約是銨離子的 30%，同時斜率被壓低 9.4%。結論很直接：用普通 NH₄Cl 標準曲線，會把 ACC 去胺酶的產物低估約 34%。

The chapter also contains a substrate-compensated standard — Solution A (ACC) titrated against Solution B (NH₄Cl) at 0/10/20/40/60/100%, giving 0 → 41.0 mM — a curve that moves the way the reaction moves. And it predicts the medium's nitrogen background from the recipe: TB and LB supernatants converge on 0.082 AU, with an A×dilution constant of 22.3 vs 9.8, a ratio of 2.27 against an expected N-source ratio of ≈2.4. 這一章還有一條基質補償標準曲線——A 液（ACC）對 B 液（NH₄Cl）以 0/10/20/40/60/100% 滴定，得到 0 → 41.0 mM——這是一條會隨著反應進程移動的曲線。它還能從培養基配方預測氮背景：TB 與 LB 上清液收斂到同一個 0.082 AU，A×稀釋倍數常數為 22.3 對 9.8，比值 2.27，而預期的氮源比值約為 2.4。

Why it matters. This is the most transferable thing the team produced: any team measuring ACC deaminase by Berthelot is systematically wrong, and this says by how much. It is a Measurement-prize argument that needs no plant to cooperate. Caveat to state aloud: two usable ACC levels, one plate, n not recorded — but the two independent estimates (33.5% and 28.1%) agree. 為什麼重要。這是全隊產出中最可被其他人沿用的東西：任何用 Berthelot 法量 ACC 去胺酶的隊伍都系統性地量錯了，而這份資料說出了錯多少。這是一個不需要植物配合就能成立的 Measurement 獎論述。必須口頭講清楚的但書：只有兩個可用的 ACC 濃度、單一盤、n 未記錄——但兩個獨立估計值（33.5% 與 28.1%）互相吻合。

2

The codon rule from the literature failed文獻上的密碼子規則失敗了

Engineering · doc Tables 6–7, in silico · strong (computational)

The greedy low-GC control was run against four genes. Minimum free energy moved WT → greedy: BoPep4 −43.20 → −48.80, ACCD −42.10 → −48.70, AmilCP −54.20 → −45.32 — and LEA14 went the wrong way, −52.60 → −48.60, with its start-region unpaired probability falling 0.512 → 0.505. The published heuristic made one of four genes worse. That failure is why the team folded ~50,000 variants instead, reaching unpaired probabilities of 0.911–0.995 at the cost of CAI dropping to 0.56–0.78. 用貪婪式低 GC 作為對照，跑了四個基因。最小自由能從野生型到貪婪版：BoPep4 −43.20 → −48.80、ACCD −42.10 → −48.70、AmilCP −54.20 → −45.32——而 LEA14 走錯了方向，−52.60 → −48.60，起始區未配對機率從 0.512 掉到 0.505。這條已發表的經驗法則在四個基因裡有一個變得更糟。正是這個失敗，讓團隊改為摺疊約 50,000 個變體，把未配對機率推到 0.911–0.995，代價是 CAI 掉到 0.56–0.78。

Why it matters. This is the cleanest "we tested a published rule, it failed, so we built our own" moment in the whole project — exactly the shape of reasoning Engineering judges reward. One slide. The wiki keeps the resulting tables but not the control that justifies them. 為什麼重要。這是整個專案中最乾淨的一個「我們測試了已發表的規則，它失敗了，所以我們自己做了一套」的時刻——這正是 Engineering 評審會獎勵的推理形狀。一張投影片就夠。Wiki 保留了最後的表格，卻沒有保留那個證成它們的對照組。

5

The soil result that argues for the whole forecast支撐整個預報概念的土耕結果

Plant page — published · Results page — missing · moderate: 2 trials, 1 pot per condition

Post-treatment ACC deaminase came in below the heat-stressed control on both fresh weight (0.103 g and 0.052 g) and chlorophyll, in both trials, and that group bolted most with the tallest stems. Pre-treatment held the most chlorophyll of the three timings in both trials. Applying the protectant too late was worse than not applying it at all — which is the argument for forecasting stress rather than reacting to it. 後處理組的 ACC 去胺酶在兩次試驗中，鮮重（0.103 g 與 0.052 g）與葉綠素都低於熱逆境對照組，而且該組抽苔最嚴重、花莖最高。前處理組在兩次試驗中都是三種時機裡葉綠素保留最多的。太晚施用保護劑，比完全不施用更糟——這正是「要預報逆境、而不是事後反應」的論據。

Correction to an earlier finding對先前一項判讀的更正

An initial pass reported this section as entirely missing from the wiki. That is true only of the Results page, which states the soil heat method and then stops. The Plant page publishes it in full, with numbers more complete than the local draft — including the honest observation that heat-stressed fresh weight rose rather than fell (0.195 g, 0.225 g), read as a developmental-stage difference rather than a benefit of heat. The evidence exists and is published; it is simply not where a judge reading Results would look. 第一輪判讀認為這整段在 wiki 上完全不見。這只對 Results 頁成立——該頁寫完土耕熱逆境的方法就停住了。Plant 頁其實完整刊出，而且數字比本地草稿更完整——包括一個誠實的觀察：熱逆境組的鮮重不降反升（0.195 g、0.225 g），並判讀為各盆發育階段的差異，而非熱逆境帶來的好處。證據是存在且已發表的，只是不在評審讀 Results 時會看的位置。

Three things to say out loud三件必須在影片裡說出口的事

Honesty risks. In each case the wiki is already careful; the video must not be less careful than the wiki.誠實風險。這三項 wiki 本身都已謹慎處理；影片的謹慎程度不可以低於 wiki。

The light switch was not shown to discriminate光開關並未被證明能區分紅綠光

At 13 h and 16 h the Level 2 induction blots show a single band between the 75 and 100 kDa markers in all four lanes, green and red alike. The wiki says so plainly: the green lanes "looked somewhat stronger… but the difference is small and has not been quantified." Earlier, red light at 100% gave expression comparable to the brightest green, which the team attributes to spectral leakage. The DNPH time course does show green above red, and that is the better evidence to lead with. 在 13 h 與 16 h，Level 2 誘導的西方墨點在四條泳道（綠光與紅光）都只顯示一條介於 75 與 100 kDa 標記之間的帶。Wiki 已經老實寫出：綠光泳道「看起來稍強……但差異很小，且未經定量」。更早之前，100% 紅光給出的表現量與最亮的綠光相當，團隊歸因於光譜洩漏。DNPH 的時間序列確實顯示綠光高於紅光，那才是比較好的主打證據。

If the video claims a working optogenetic switch on the basis of a Western blot, a judge who opens the wiki will find the team's own caveat. Lead with DNPH activity; describe the blots as supporting, not demonstrating. 如果影片以西方墨點為依據宣稱光遺傳開關「有效」，打開 wiki 的評審會讀到團隊自己寫的但書。請以 DNPH 活性為主論據，把墨點描述為旁證，而非證明。

Asterisks that are not biological replicates不是生物重複的星號

On the agar sets, each bar is three reads of one pooled extract from one plate per condition. The significance marks are therefore technical, not biological. Separately, agar sets 9–11 compare against TB medium rather than supernatant from the strain without the protectant; only the hydroponic runs carried that WB800 control — which makes hydroponic BoPep4 at 1/40 the strongest proof-of-concept the project has, still from a single run. 在 agar 各組中，每一根長條是同一盤、同一管混合萃取液的三次讀值。因此那些顯著性標記是技術重複，不是生物重複。另外，agar 第 9–11 組是對照 TB 培養基，而不是對照「沒有保護劑的同一菌株」的上清液；只有水耕組帶了 WB800 這個對照——這使得水耕 1/40 BoPep4 成為全專案最強的概念驗證，儘管仍然只有單次試驗。

The sprayed batch has no activity assay behind it噴灑用的那批蛋白沒有活性佐證

The 24 August Western blot lane for the 8/23 production batch is blank in the notebook. That batch went into the set-8 pretest and the early soil pretest, yet the wiki describes set 8 as using "bioreactor-produced ACC deaminase." No plant run in the corpus is tied to an activity measurement of the material actually applied. 實驗記錄本中，8 月 24 日針對 8/23 生產批次的西方墨點泳道是空白的。那一批進了第 8 組預試與早期土耕預試，但 wiki 將第 8 組描述為使用「生物反應器生產的 ACC 去胺酶」。在整份資料裡，沒有任何一次植物試驗能連結到「實際施用的那批material」的活性測定。

This is the single weakest joint in the end-to-end story — bioreactor to plant. If the video narrates an unbroken chain, that joint is where a judge's question will land. Saying it first is cheaper than being asked. 這是「生物反應器到植物」這條端到端故事中最弱的一個接點。如果影片敘述一條不間斷的鏈條，評審的問題就會落在這個接點上。自己先講出來，比被問到便宜得多。

One open question一個未解的問題
