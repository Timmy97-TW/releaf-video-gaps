The ELISA and DNPH plate data do not show ACC deaminase above a negative control, and the wiki says so itself, twice. On the DNPH variants plot the plasmid-free WB800 bar (~32) sits above csn (~21); only the +PLP bars clear it, so the figure demonstrates a PLP requirement, not ACCD activity. What does survive — and it is enough真正站得住的證據——而且夠用

The bioreactor permeate is the honest proof that you made working enzyme. It is activity normalised to concentration, measured in a cell-free compartment on the far side of the membrane: specific activity below 1 U/mg at 6–12 h, rising to 6.3 U/mg at 18 h and holding 5–8.4 U/mg through 72 h. Cell-free, so it cannot be host background. That is a stronger statement than any plate bar chart, and it ties claims 2 and 4 together into one result. 生物反應器的透過液才是「你們做出有功能的酵素」的誠實證據。它是以濃度正規化後的活性，而且量在膜另一側的無細胞區間：比活性在 6–12 h 低於 1 U/mg，升到 18 h 的 6.3 U/mg，並在 72 h 之前維持 5–8.4 U/mg。無細胞，所以不可能是宿主背景。這個敘述比任何微量盤長條圖都強，而且把主張 2 和主張 4 綁成同一個結果。

If you have 30 seconds spare, use it here如果多出 30 秒，用在這裡

The deleted Berthelot finding is the most transferable thing the team produced and appears nowhere on the wiki: ACC is itself chromogenic at roughly 30% of ammonium, so a plain NH₄Cl calibration underestimates ACC-deaminase product by about 34%. Any team measuring ACC deaminase this way is systematically wrong. It needs one slide and no plant to cooperate — and it is a Measurement-prize argument that nothing else in the video competes with. 被刪掉的 Berthelot 發現是全隊最能被他人沿用的成果，而 wiki 上完全看不到：ACC 本身就會顯色，顯色能力約為銨離子的 30%，所以普通 NH₄Cl 標準曲線會把 ACC 去胺酶的產物低估約 34%。任何用這個方法量 ACC 去胺酶的隊伍都系統性地量錯了。它只需要一張投影片，而且不需要任何植物配合——這是一個 Measurement 獎論述，影片裡沒有別的內容能與它競爭。
