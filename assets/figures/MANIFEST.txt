NOTES FOR THE EDIT
======================================================================
1. Sixteen of the 32 files are under 900 px wide. Every one of them is a
   plot or an assay chart, not a photograph, so the cleanest fix is to
   re-export them from the plotting source at 1920 px or wider rather
   than upscaling these.
2. The four light-exposure panels (202-268 px) are the weakest in the
   pack. At full screen they will visibly pixelate. Use them only as
   small multiples in a grid, or re-export them.
3. reactor-r2-sio2-calibration.svg is the one file with no resolution
   ceiling. Favour it where a clean full-screen data slide is needed.
4. Five of the six local "HIRES" originals do beat their wiki copies
   (2x to 3x). The exception is the P5 rig photo, flagged above.
